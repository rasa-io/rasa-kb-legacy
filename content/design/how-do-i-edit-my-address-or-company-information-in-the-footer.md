According to [CAN-SPAM laws](https://www.ftc.gov/tips-advice/business-center/guidance/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), a business address is required in the footer for commercial or business-related marketing emails.   
  
You can edit the auto-populated information you see in the Footer section by going to [Manage Settings -> Newsletter Settings -> Name & Company](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings/company) and changing the address and also the company name there.

![]()![mceclip0 (16)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/mceclip0%20(16).png)

If you work from home or do not have an actual office, we do have suggestions below for an alternative address.

- Use a P.O Box

- Use a business manager's address or other business representation

- Use the address of an affiliated partner such as a store, gallery, or studio
