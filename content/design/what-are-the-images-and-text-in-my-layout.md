You can add static content in the form of images and text to your newsletter by going to [Design](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design) and clicking the [*Layout*](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design#layout) tab. You can [drag and drop](https://help.rasa.io/how-does-layout-work) all of these modules within the layout to fall wherever you'd like in the template.

The **Text/HTML** module is a place for you to infuse a text-based message to your subscribers. You can use our editor to enter and style your text or you can select the HTML tab and drop in your very own code. We only recommend using the HTML option if you are well-versed in HTML formatting. This text can include hyperlinks as well.

We also have an [integration with ChatGPT](https://help.rasa.io/chatgpt-beta-feature) to help you write if you so choose. Simply Click **Generate with ChatGPT,** provide the prompt and customizations of your choosing, and generate!

Two **banner images** and two **square images** are also available for use. You can use images to further brand your newsletter and promote your website, events, or anything relevant to your audience! If you do not want to include a given image in your upcoming newsletter, you can either delete the image that is currently uploaded or just click the eyeball icon on the module to hide it.

The recommended sizes for these are 250 x 250 for the square images and 600 x 150 for the banner images. If you need to resize an image to meet these recommendations, we suggest using [https://www.pixlr.com](https://pixlr.com/).
