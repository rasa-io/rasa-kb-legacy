The **From Name** is the name that appears in your subscribers’ inboxes as who the newsletter is coming from, while the **From Email Address** is the email address the newsletter is sent from.

The default From Email Address is **thiswas@sentwithrasa.io**.

You can update the **From Name** on the **Preview page**, under **Send Settings**, to the name of your choosing.

To change the **From Email Address** to an address that you manage, you must first authenticate your domain. We highly recommend sending from an email address that your contacts are familiar with—this helps reduce the chances of your newsletter landing in spam folders and increases the likelihood that it reaches your subscribers’ inboxes.

**Note:** We are not able to send from personal email accounts such as `gmail.com`, `yahoo.com`, and similar providers.
