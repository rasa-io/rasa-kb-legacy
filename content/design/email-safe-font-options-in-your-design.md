_Why am I limited to certain fonts for my template?_

We strive to ensure your emails are delivered and displayed consistently across various email clients. Due to the limitations imposed by certain email platforms, we offer a curated selection of email safe fonts to maintain optimal readability and compatibility.   
  
The below fonts have been tested and proven to render correctly across a wide range of devices and email clients, ensuring your message reaches your audience effectively. The first group (Email Safe) is among the most common email safe fonts, whereas the second (Custome) will not always be guaranteed across all clients. If a certain client does not support one of the below options, it will typically fall back to Arial or Helvetica, but can still vary beyond that.
