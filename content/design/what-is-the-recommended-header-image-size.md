_Ensure your header image looks sharp by using the correct size and resolution. This guide covers recommended dimensions, tips for fixing blurry images, and custom template considerations._

###### **Recommended Header Image Size**

The general recommended size for header images is **150 x 600**. Using this size ensures your image is displayed without any pixelation or distortion issues.

###### **Troubleshooting Fuzzy or Blurry Header Images**

If your header image appears fuzzy or pixelated:

1. **Check the Image Dimensions:** Ensure the image matches the recommended dimensions (150x600 pixels) or any custom size specified for your template.
2. **Resize the Image:** If the dimensions do not match, use an image resizer tool, such as [ImageResizer.com](https://www.imageresizer.com).
   - Upload your image to the tool.
   - Adjust the dimensions to the recommended size.
   - Download the resized image.
3. **Reload the Resized Image:** After resizing, upload the adjusted image to your newsletter and preview it for clarity.

###### **Custom Header Image Sizes for Templates**

If you’re using a custom template that requires a header image size different from the general recommendation, ensure the image you provide matches the exact dimensions specified for your template.

- Example: If your custom template requires a 200x800-pixel image, resizing your header image to this size will help maintain its quality and clarity.

**Note:** Providing images with dimensions that do not align with the template requirements may result in pixelation or stretching.

###### **Best Practices for Clear Header Images**

- Always use high-resolution images before resizing.
- Avoid excessive compression, as it can degrade image quality.
- Preview your newsletter after uploading the header image to confirm its appearance.

By following these guidelines, you can ensure that your header images enhance your newsletter's professional look. If you encounter any issues, feel free to reach out for further assistance!
