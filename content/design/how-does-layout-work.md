If you are using one of our four standard templates, you can easily edit the layout of the templates within the Templates section of your dashboard.

To edit the template you’re using, navigate to **Templates**, then select **Edit** on the template you’d like to modify. Any changes you make there will apply to newsletters using that template.

Within the **Layout** tab of the **Design** section, you can arrange the various elements of your template using a simple drag-and-drop approach, ensuring the layout aligns with your newsletter needs.

###### Adding Modules

You can add new modules to your layout by clicking the **plus (+) icon** in the **top-right corner** of the Layout tab. From there, select the module you’d like to add—such as an **Article**, **Banner**, **Square**, **Text/HTML**, and more.

###### Rearranging Modules

To rearrange modules within the template, hover over the **two bars on the left-hand side** of any module to select it, then drag and drop it into the position of your choosing.

![dragndrop](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/dragndrop.gif)  

###### Hiding Modules

If you don’t need to use a module, click the **eye icon** to hide it. If a module like a banner, text/HTML block, or square has no content, it will automatically collapse and not display in the email.

###### Editing Module Content

To edit the content within a module, simply click on it to open the editor or upload page. You can also control how many articles appear in the initial article section of your template.

![dragndroparticles](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/dragndroparticles.gif)

###### Sections in Your Newsletter

If your newsletter includes **sections**, each section will appear as its **own module** within the Layout tab. You can click directly into the section you want to edit to:

- Adjust how many articles appear in that section
- Customize the section header design
