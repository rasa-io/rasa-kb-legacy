Removing our branding from the footer of your newsletter is simple and straightforward. Follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the [Design Page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design):** Access the design settings for your newsletter.
2. **Select [Footer](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design#foote):** Locate the footer section within the design settings.
3. **Toggle Branding:** Within the footer settings, you'll find an option to toggle our branding on or off. Simply switch it off to remove our branding from your newsletter footer.

That's it! Your newsletter will now reflect your desired branding without any additional logos or information.
