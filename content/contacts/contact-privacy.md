Here at rasa.io, we know that your contacts are **your** contacts. rasa.io will not sell your contacts' email addresses or data nor will rasa.io distribute them in any way shape or form. We will not access your contacts unless necessary to assist in troubleshooting a problem. We respect your privacy and do not share your information with anyone.

To read our full privacy policy, please click [here](https://rasa.io/privacy/).
