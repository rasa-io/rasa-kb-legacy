If you would like to unsubscribe multiple contacts from your list at once, go to the [Bulk Unsubscribe](https://dashboard.rasa.io/contacts/bulk-unsubscribe) page. You will be able to upload a CSV file with the emails that you would like unsubscribed.

![screenshot-dashboard.rasa.io-2020.06 (5)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/screenshot-dashboard.rasa.io-2020.06%20(5).png)

You also have the option to [bulk archive](https://dashboard.rasa.io/contacts/bulk-archive) contacts if you would like them completely deleted from your newsletter and contact count.
