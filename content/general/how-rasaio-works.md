Welcome to the new way of sending newsletters! With rasa.io, you can create your smart newsletter in just a few easy steps. Then, you'll be well on your way to automatically sending a personalized email that people want to read. So, how does it work?

![](https://fast.wistia.com/embed/medias/pht6nrhbep/swatch)

**Design your email template.**

As you may have seen in our [Get Started](https://getstarted.rasa.io) process, you can play around with our template designs before you even create an account! However keep in mind, once you do create an account, you'll have *many* more options to customize it further in your [Design tab](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design).

**Now it's up to you to add the articles of your choice!**

We'll autofill your design page with our fun stock content but you'll want to turn those into real, relevant news stories for your audience by adding sources and articles that make sense for your audience.

**How? [Add sources](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/addsource).**

A source is any website (via RSS) or social media channel (Facebook, YouTube, or Linkedin) that publishes or shares articles you think your audience would be interested in. Not sure where to start? Check out our awesome [library of sources for all industries and topics](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/library).

We'll automatically bring the articles they publish into your [Upcoming](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) articles pool. You can always edit, remove, and [add articles](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/addarticle) in the dashboard too. We will choose the best articles for each of your subscribers and send each person their own, personalized newsletter.

**What happens next?**

Once you have articles flowing in, just set your send schedule and you can sit back and let our AI do the work. Each subscriber will get a personalized newsletter with a unique selection of articles from your [Upcoming](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) pool.

**What if I want to feature certain articles instead of having the AI choose?**

No problem! The beauty of rasa.io is that you can feature your content first, alongside the personalized news. Just use our [Boost](https://help.rasa.io/what-does-the-boost-lightning-bolt-button-do) feature to automate that. You can have any level of AI personalization that feels right to you.

**What if I want to add plain text or images - not just articles?**

[We got you](https://help.rasa.io/adding-your-own-text-images-or-advertisements).
