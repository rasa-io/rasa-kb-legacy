![]()![mceclip4](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/mceclip4.png)Super [Boost](https://help.rasa.io/what-does-the-boost-lightning-bolt-button-do) - an article or all articles from a boosted source will be in every subscriber's newsletter and this will ensure that it is at the top of each newsletter. To super boost an article or a source, you can double-click the lightning bolt icon next to the [source](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/sources) or the [article on the upcoming page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content).

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/cdn.zeplin.io5d6023941db0449b12f2de7fassets08C7B974-BF85-4361-BEC6-B62E7DC5EE3C-2.svg)        [Boost](https://help.rasa.io/what-does-the-boost-lightning-bolt-button-do) - an article or all articles from a boosted source will be in every subscriber's newsletter.  When an article has been boosted by you, it overrides the AI and ensures all subscribers will see it. You can boost an article or a source. To boost an article or a source, you can select the icon next to the [source](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/sources) or the [article on the upcoming page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content).

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/cdn.zeplin.io5d6023941db0449b12f2de7fassetsE786E3FF-8336-490F-A05A-CF27DB4270F0.svg)   Toggle On - indicates that it is eligible and could be included in your newsletter. You can toggle on sources, articles, and filters.

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/cdn.zeplin.io5d6023941db0449b12f2de7fassetsAC2420F4-B205-4DE7-A6A5-51C79376029A.svg)   Toggle Off - indicates that either a source, article, or filter is turned off making it ineligible for a newsletter.

\*Note: if a source is toggled off, it is still included in your total source count.

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/cdn.zeplin.io5d6023941db0449b12f2de7fassets5293F257-F385-40AC-97DC-0BE6F5971EF2.svg)     Image or text in the template will display

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Knowledge%20Base%20Import/cdn.zeplin.io5d6023941db0449b12f2de7fassets626C3A86-8A3F-4CEF-8922-0B07BFDB8E19.svg)      Image or text in the template will not display
