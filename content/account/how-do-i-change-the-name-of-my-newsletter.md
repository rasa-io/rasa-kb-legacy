In the rasa.io dashboard, you have the flexibility to customize the name of your newsletter. Follow these simple steps to change the name of your newsletter:

1. **Access Manage Settings:**

   - Log in to your rasa.io account.
   - On the left-hand side menu, click on [Manage Settings](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings).
2. **Navigate to Newsletter Settings:**

   - Within the [Manage Settings](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings), locate and click on Newsletter Settings.
3. **Select Name & Company:**

   - In the Newsletter Settings page, find the [Name & Company](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings/company) option and click on it.
4. **Update Newsletter Name:**

   - Under [Name & Company](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings/company), you will see the "Newsletter Name" field.
   - Enter your desired name for the newsletter in the provided text box.
5. **Save Changes:**

   - Once you've entered the new name, don't forget to click the "Save" button to apply the changes.

*Note: If you wish to change the "From" name and/or email address associated with your newsletter, please refer to [this page](https://help.rasa.io/changing-your-from-name-and-from-email-address-in-send-settings) on that topic.*

That's it! Your newsletter name is now updated. If you have any questions or encounter any issues while changing the newsletter name, feel free to reach out to our support team for assistance.
