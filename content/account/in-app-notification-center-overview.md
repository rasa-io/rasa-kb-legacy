_The In-App Notification Center is a centralized hub within the dashboard that allows you to stay updated on important events, actions, and announcements without leaving the app._

###### Key Features of the Notification Center

- **Real-Time Notifications**: Instantly receive updates related to your account, activities, or system-wide announcements as they happen.
- **Actionable Messages**: Direct links to actions such as reviewing updates, approving requests, or accessing new features.
- **Categorized Notifications**: Notifications are sorted by category (e.g., updates, reminders, warnings) to make it easy to find what you're looking for.
- **Read/Unread Status**: Clearly see which notifications you've already reviewed and which ones are new, helping you keep track of tasks.

###### How to Access the Notification Center

1. **Locating the Notification Icon**: The Notification Center can be accessed by clicking the bell icon located at the top-right corner of the dashboard.  
   ![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/image-png-Oct-04-2024-03-04-18-5927-PM.png)
2. **Viewing Notifications**: When you have new notifications, the icon will display a badge with the number of unread notifications.  ![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/image-png-Oct-04-2024-03-14-11-5580-PM.png) Click the icon to open the Notification Center.
3. **Interacting with Notifications**:
   - **Mark as Read**: Once you've reviewed a notification, you can mark it as read to remove it from your unread list.  
     ![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/image-png-Oct-04-2024-03-19-12-3262-PM.png)

- **Take Action**: Some notifications allow you to take direct actions, like approving a request or completing a task with just one click.

###### Common Notifications

- **System Updates**: Receive alerts when important system maintenance is scheduled.
- **Dashboard Updates**: Recieve alerts when new updates are made to the dashboard and/or features are released.
- **Content Review:** Recieve an alert when your content is ready for review for an upcoming send.
- **Schedule is paused:** Receive an alert if your newsletter schedule has been paused.
- **Integration Disconnect:** Receive alerts when integrations have been disconnected and need attention.
- **CSV Integration:** Receive alerts regarding recent CSV imports.

###### FAQ

**Q: Can I receive notifications outside of the app?**  
A: Yes! If you want to receive notifications via email, you can enable these options in your [Settings > Preferences > Email Notifications](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings/emailpreferences).

**Q: How can I find old notifications?**  
A: You can access older notifications by scrolling down within the Notification Center.
