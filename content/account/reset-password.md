To reset your password, visit <https://dashboard.rasa.io/> and click 'Forgot Password?'

![]()

You'll then receive an email from rasa.io.  If you do not receive this email within an hour or two, please check your junk or spam folder.

Contact [support](mailto:%20support@rasa.io) if you need additional assistance.
