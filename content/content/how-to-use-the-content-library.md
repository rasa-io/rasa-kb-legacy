The [Content Library](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/library) can be found on the [Add a New Source page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/addsource) and is a great tool for discovering new relevant sources for content in your newsletter.

![]()![mceclip0 (4)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/mceclip0%20(4).png)

You can filter the [Content Library](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/library) by category and browse hundreds of different RSS feeds from a wide array of online publications.

![Screen Recording 2020-10-30 at 11.01.24.55 AM](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Screen%20Recording%202020-10-30%20at%2011.01.24.55%20AM.gif)

Once you find a source that you want to add to [your sources](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/sources), just click the plus (+) button and it will directly add the feed to your [sources page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/sources).

As soon as you add that source, we'll start pulling in their latest published articles to your [Upcoming Content](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) which will make it eligible for your newsletter!

If you want to learn more about RSS feeds, visit [this page.](https://help.rasa.io/finding-an-rss-feed) To better understand how sources work at rasa.io, please click [here](https://help.rasa.io/how-do-sources-work)!
