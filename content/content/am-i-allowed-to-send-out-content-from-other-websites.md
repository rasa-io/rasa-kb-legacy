**How does rasa.io gather content?**

The rasa.io system gathers and pulls content for your newsletter by using publicly available feeds such as RSS, Facebook, YouTube, and more. These feeds are provided by the publishers of the respective websites, allowing rasa.io to aggregate and distribute their content in different ways.

**Am I allowed to send out content from other websites?**

Yes, you are absolutely allowed to send out content from other websites! In fact, incorporating content from external sources is a valuable method to create and curate engaging newsletters for your subscribers. As mentioned above, the articles displayed on your [upcoming articles page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) are published to the feeds and rasa.io will bring in those articles exactly as they are published. rasa.io will always use the page URLs provided by the publisher so that all readers are directed back to the original source once they click on an article in your newsletter.

If you have any further questions or concerns about the use of external content in your newsletters, please do not hesitate to contact us at [support@rasa.io](mailto:support@rasa.io). Our team will be happy to assist you.

Additionally, if you're interested in learning more about how RSS feeds work, you can find further information on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSS) and you can guidance on how to find an RSS feed [here](https://help.rasa.io/finding-an-rss-feed).

Note - We take copyrights seriously and aim to comply with all regulations. rasa.io directs traffic to publishers to support and avoid infringement. We aim to ensure responsible content sharing.
