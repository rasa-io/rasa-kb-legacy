The sources that you add to the dashboard are where the content for your newsletter will come from. rasa.io pulls in articles published on your sources into the [upcoming articles page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) to aggregate content for your newsletter.

Types of Sources and how they work:

- **RSS Feed ![]()** - This is the most common source used on our platform. Most website publish their articles to a feed so that platforms like rasa.io can aggregate them for readers! Check out this [page](https://help.rasa.io/finding-an-rss-feed) to learn more about RSS feeds and how to find them.
- **Facebook ![]()**- We will pull in URLs posted to a Facebook page as long as the page is public.
- **YouTube ![]()**- We will pull in the links to your latest videos when you enter your channel ID as a source. For more information on how to use YouTube as a source, please see [here](https://help.rasa.io/how-to-add-a-youtube-channel-as-source).
- **Linkedin ![]()**-  We will pull in URLs posted to your organization's LinkedIn page. To use LinkedIn, it must be your organization's account.
- **Google News ![]() -**This is a great option for a source as you can enter a Google News feed on a given topic and we will pull in content that is published on that feed and is relevant to the topic. Please see more information about [Google News as a source](https://help.rasa.io/how-to-add-a-google-news-feed-as-a-source) for your newsletter.

To add any given type of source, visit the Add a New Source page, choose the correct type of source, and enter the necessary information (RSS feed, Facebook page URL, YouTube Channel ID, etc.). You have the option [to rename and edit a source](https://help.rasa.io/editing-a-source) as well once it is added. You can also check out our [Content Library](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/library) for more sources.

![]()The dashboard will continuously pull in articles from your sources several times per day. You can even manually "refresh" your sources on demand by clicking the 3 dots you see on the [sources page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content/sources). The pulls will pause once your [cutoff review window](https://help.rasa.io/what-is-the-content-review-cut-off-time) begins and resume after your next newsletter is sent.![]()

![mceclip1 (3)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/mceclip1%20(3).png)

As soon as your newsletter is sent, the articles that came in after the cutoff will appear and new articles will continue to pull in as they are published. [Click here](http://22539073.hs-sites.com/en/rasaioknowledge/how-upcoming-articles-works) to learn about all of the other features on your [Upcoming page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content).

\*Note - We take copyrights seriously and aim to comply with all regulations. rasa.io directs traffic to publishers to support and avoid infringement. We aim to ensure responsible content sharing.
