Scheduled Content allows you to plan ahead by scheduling articles, images, and text to appear in specific sends. This is especially useful if you’ve already created graphics for your promotional areas and don’t want to manually update them before each send — simply schedule them once, and they’ll appear automatically at the right time.

To schedule images or text:

1. Go to the **Schedule** page and click **Scheduled Content** in the left navigation.
2. Click **Add**, then choose **Image** or **Text**.

   - For **images**, select where the image will appear, where it should link, and set the run dates.
   - For **text**, follow the same process to set when it should display.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT:**  
**You must enable scheduled content in your layout settings.**  
Go to your **Layout** page and toggle **Scheduled Content** (the calendar icon) to **ON**.

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/image-png-Nov-05-2025-04-25-03-0840-PM.png)  
If this is not enabled, your scheduled images and text **will not appear** in your newsletter.
