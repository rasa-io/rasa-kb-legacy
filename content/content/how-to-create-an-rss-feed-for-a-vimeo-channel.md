In order to create an RSS feed for a Vimeo channel, follow the steps below:

1. Go to the Vimeo channel you want to create an RSS feed for.

2. Look at the page URL. For example:

[https://vimeo.com/georgiafootball/](https://vimeo.com/georgiafootball/videos/rss)

3. Place this value at the end of the URL: /videos/RSS

Your RSS feed URL should look like this:

<https://vimeo.com/georgiafootball/videos/rss>

![]()

You should also see a link to the RSS feed on the /videos page on the right bottom side:
