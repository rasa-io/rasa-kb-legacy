_The "Add Topic as a Source" feature allows you to enhance your newsletter by pulling relevant content correlated to a topic._

###### Benefits of Adding Topics as Sources

1. **Content Relevance**: Automatically source content that matches your audience's interests.
2. **Time Savings**: Reduce the time spent searching for material to include in your newsletter.
3. **Diverse Sources**: Integrate a variety of articles and updates to enrich your content.

---

###### How It Works

1. **Choose a Topic**  
   Select a topic that aligns with your newsletter. For example, you can choose "Technology," "Health," or "AI Trends."
2. **Source Integration**  
   The system will pull content from trusted sources related to the topic.
3. **Content Curation**  
   Review the curated content.

###### Pro Tips

1. **Focus on Niche Topics**: Selecting highly specific topics helps ensure unique, valuable content for your readers.
2. **Review Curated Content Regularly**: Automation is great, but adding a personal touch ensures your newsletter maintains quality and alignment with your goals.

---

###### Frequently Asked Questions

###### Can I add more than one topic as a source?

Depending on your plan, you will have a specific cap on the number of topics as sources.

Plus plans have 1 topic source max.

Pro plans have 3 topic sources max.

Enterprise plans have 6 topic sources max.

###### What types of content will be sourced?

The system can pull articles, blogs, videos, and other content based on your topic and filter preferences.

###### What happens if the topic doesn’t yield relevant content?

You can refine your topic or filters to improve content relevance.
