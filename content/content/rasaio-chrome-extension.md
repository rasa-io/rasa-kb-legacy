rasa.io has a Google Chrome extension which allows you to easily add articles to your [upcoming articles](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) page while you are browsing the web! Simply click on the rasa.io icon ![blobid0](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/blobid0.png)as you're visiting the article's URL to add it to your newsletter's content pool.

To add the Chrome extension, follow these steps...

1. Visit the [rasa.io Chrome Extension page](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/rasaio/jkooeebkmkghhhmohahoaifamgaecakj?hl=en)

2. Click 'Add to Chrome'

![screenshot-chrome.google.com-2020.04 (2)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/screenshot-chrome.google.com-2020.04%20(2).png)

3. You will be prompted to input your login information for your rasa.io account so that the articles can be sent to your newsletter's [upcoming content pool](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content) when using the rasa.io extension.

![screenshot-chrome.google.com-2020.04 (3)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/screenshot-chrome.google.com-2020.04%20(3).png)

4. Once the extension is added to Google Chrome and have entered your login information, you should see ![blobid0](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/blobid0.png) at the top of your browser alongside your other extensions.

When you are viewing an article or page that you would like to share with your newsletter subscribers, just click the ![blobid0](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/blobid0.png) and the article will be sent to your [upcoming articles page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/content)!
