[Active Subscriber Report](https://dashboard.rasa.io/analytics/active-subscribers)

**Definition**

**Monthly Active Subscribers (MAS)** are the number of unique subscribers who engage with your newsletter content during a selected time period. This metric highlights how many people are actively interacting with your emails, offering a clearer picture of audience health than list size alone.

**Purpose**

Tracking MAS helps you:

- Understand trends in subscriber activity and retention.

- Measure newsletter growth over time.

- Identify patterns of engagement across different time periods.

This metric works like a “fitness score” for your audience: steady or increasing MAS indicates strong, consistent engagement, while declines may point to issues in content, cadence, or deliverability.

**Reporting Options**

The graphs in this report allow you to choose how data is grouped:

- **Daily** – view changes on a day-to-day basis.

- **Weekly** – see broader weekly trends.

- **Monthly** – track long-term growth and retention.

Changing the grouping interval updates both graphs and the summary table.

**Graphs in the Report**

**Active Subscribers Over Time**

- Displays the total number of active subscribers for each selected period.

- Use grouping options to zoom in on daily fluctuations or step back for longer trends.

**Average Interactions per Active Subscriber**

- Shows the average number of interactions (opens, clicks) per active subscriber.
- Helps gauge the depth of engagement within your active audience.

**Data Table**

The summary table combines both datapoints into a single view:

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Date** | **Active Subscribers** | **Total Engagements** | **Avg Engagement/Subscriber** |
| Aug 5, 2025 | 1,434 | 8,453 | 5.89 |

- **Active Subscribers** – number of unique subscribers who engaged during the time period.

- **Total Engagements** – total number of interactions (opens, clicks).

- **Avg Engagement/Subscriber** – the average interactions per active subscriber, showing engagement depth.

**Export Options**

The complete report can be exported as **PDF or Excel**, making it easy to share with your team or track results over time.
