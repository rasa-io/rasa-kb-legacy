###### What does ISO certification mean, and why does it matter?

The **International Organization for Standardization (ISO)** sets international benchmarks for quality, safety, and efficiency. Earning an ISO certification means that a company meets these best practices, ensuring the highest level of quality in its products and services.

For data privacy, specific standards such as **ISO 27001** create a framework for robust information security management systems (ISMS). This standard outlines how organizations can protect data through risk assessment, access controls, incident management, and continuous improvement.

**Why ISO certification matters**:  
Achieving ISO certification provides organizations with a formal structure to manage risks tied to information security. At rasa.io, this means we help our clients build trust with their subscribers by proving our commitment to responsible data handling.
