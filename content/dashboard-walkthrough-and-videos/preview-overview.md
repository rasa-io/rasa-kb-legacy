The Preview Page is a great way to preview what an upcoming send will look like for your contacts. It will marry the content and design page together so that you will see any boosted articles, any scheduled content, and more. Keep in mind that the preview will vary depending on the AI selection of content. This is the best way to send yourself tests before an upcoming send and make sure everything looks good!

**Note\*\*** You also can preview and send a test email from the [Test Email](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design#sendpreview) tab on the [Design page](https://dashboard.rasa.io/design) as well. This option will show you the design of your newsletter - not the content.
