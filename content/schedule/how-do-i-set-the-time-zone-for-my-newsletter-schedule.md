---
title: How do I set the time zone for my newsletter schedule?
source: https://help.rasa.io/how-do-i-set-the-time-zone-for-my-newsletter-schedule
---

You can change the time zone for your account and schedule under the company profile under [Manage Settings](https://dashboard.rasa.io/settings) > Name & Company > Time Zone.

![]()![Smart-Newsletter-Dashboard-rasa-io (12) (1)](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/Smart-Newsletter-Dashboard-rasa-io%20(12)%20(1).png)

Once you change the time zone, you will see the change reflected on the [Schedule](https://dashboard.rasa.io/schedule) page next to the delivery time.
