We’ve launched the [**rasa.io Learning Hub & Community**](https://community.rasa.io), a centralized space for education, troubleshooting, and peer connection — all designed to help you get the most out of your rasa.io newsletter.

###### What You Can Do in the Hub

Learn how to use rasa.io more effectively  
Browse guided courses and tutorials that cover everything from platform basics to advanced strategies. Ideal for both new users and teams onboarding new members.

Get faster answers to common questions  
Access a growing knowledge base and searchable support content. For anything not covered, post in the community to hear from the rasa.io team or other users.

Connect with peers  
Join discussions around shared challenges, strategies, and use cases. You can comment under course content or start new threads in the community space.

Stay informed  
Check in for the latest platform updates, feature releases, and system status announcements — all posted in real time.

###### How to Access

Go to [**community.rasa.io**](https://community.rasa.io) to explore the hub. Having trouble logging in? Contact support@rasa.io for help.
