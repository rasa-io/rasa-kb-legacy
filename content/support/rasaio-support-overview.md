###### Knowledge Base

Our comprehensive Knowledge Base is available across all plans, providing you with valuable resources to enhance your experience. Explore it [here](https://app.hubspot.com/knowledge/22539073/edit/152893051154).

###### Community

Connect with other users, share insights, and stay updated on the latest developments within our vibrant community.

###### Support Ticketing

For more personalized assistance, all plans come with support ticketing options. Simply submit a ticket by emailing us at support@rasa.io or using the Support in-app widget from your [dashboard](https://dashboard.rasa.io/).

###### Response Times

We understand the importance of timely support. Our response times vary based on your plan, ensuring that your concerns are addressed promptly.

- **Plus:** Expect a response within 72 hours.
- **Pro:** Enjoy a quicker turnaround with responses within 48 hours.
- **Enterprise:** Experience expedited support with responses within 24 hours.

We appreciate your trust in rasa.io, and our team is here to ensure your journey with us is smooth and successful. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to reach out!
