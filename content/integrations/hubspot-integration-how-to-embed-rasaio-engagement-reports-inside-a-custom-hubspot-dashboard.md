---
title: HubSpot Integration: How to embed rasa.io engagement reports inside a custom HubSpot dashboard
source: https://help.rasa.io/hubspot-integration-how-to
keywords: hubspot,hubspot integration,engagement report
---

Step 1: Go to whatever dashboard you want to embed these engagement reports on

Step 2: Click the 'Actions' drop-down from the top right and select 'Add external content'

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/undefined-Nov-11-2024-07-14-47-2468-PM.png)

Step 3: Choose 'Other content' and add a title and then paste the URL of the engagement report

\* The URLs for the engagement reports specific to your newsletter can be found on the integration page within the rasa.io dashboard\*

![](https://22539073.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/22539073/undefined-Nov-11-2024-07-14-48-0763-PM.png)
