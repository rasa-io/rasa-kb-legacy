How do I edit my address or company information in the footer?

📁 Design View original ↗
CAN-SPAM complianceedit addresscompany informationfooter settingsnewsletter settingsfooter customizationcompany nameemail footercontact informationmanage settings

According to CAN-SPAM laws, a business address is required in the footer for commercial or business-related marketing emails.

You can edit the auto-populated information you see in the Footer section by going to Manage Settings -> Newsletter Settings -> Name & Company and changing the address and also the company name there.

If you work from home or do not have an actual office, we do have suggestions below for an alternative address.

  • Use a P.O Box

  • Use a business manager's address or other business representation

  • Use the address of an affiliated partner such as a store, gallery, or studio
