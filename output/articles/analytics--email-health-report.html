Email Health Report

📁 Analytics View original ↗
email health report

The Email Health Report evaluates the performance of your newsletter across key metrics. Each metric is assigned a score based on predefined benchmarks, and an Overall Health Score is calculated.

Review your email health report
  • In your account, navigate to Analytics> Email Health Report
  • Click Email Health Report from the left side of the navigation.
  • Your email health will be summarized by an overall health score, which is based on an aggregate of your newsletter metrics. This health score is percentile-based, which maps to an assessment that's based on your numerical score:
    • 1 - 2: Poor
    • 3 - 4:Needs work
    • 5 - 6: Okay
    • 7 - 8: Good
    • 8: Very good
    • 9 - 10: Excellent
  • Your aggregate engagement metrics, including Opens, Click-throughs, Hard bounces, Spam reports, and Unsubscribes are provided at the top of the email health dashboard, along with the total number of emails you've sent in the last 30 days.

Scoring System

Each metric is scored based on performance relative to the benchmarks:

Performance Score
Excellent 10
Very Good 8
Good 6
Okay 4
Needs Work 2
Poor 0

Example:

  • Open Rate: 35% → Very Good → Score = 8
  • CTR: 1.8% → Good → Score = 6
  • Hard Bounce: 2% → Very Good → Score = 8
  • Soft Bounce: 2.5% → Good → Score = 6

Total Score = 8 + 6 + 8 + 6 = 28
Average Score = 28 ÷ 4 = 7

Overall Health Score

The Overall Health Score is calculated as the average of all metric scores. This score determines the campaign's overall performance category:

Score Range Overall Health
1 - 2 Poor
3 - 4 Needs Work
5 - 6 Okay
7 - 8 Good
9 - 10 Excellent

Example Analysis:

  1. Opens: 35% → Very Good → Score = 8
  2. CTR: 1.8% → Good → Score = 6
  3. Hard Bounce: 2% → Very Good → Score = 8
  4. Soft Bounce: 2.5% → Good → Score = 6

Total Score = 28
Average Score = 7
Overall Health: Good

Key Takeaways
  • Monitor key metrics to identify underperforming areas.
  • Strive for Excellent or Very Good benchmarks in all metrics for high newsletter health.
  • Regularly review the Overall Health Score to track improvements over time.

This report offers actionable insights into improving email performance and ensuring effective engagement with your audience.
