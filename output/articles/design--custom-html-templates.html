Custom HTML Templates

📁 Design View original ↗

The option to use or create your own custom HTML template is available to you. You can upload the HTML file in the Widgets & Tools section. Custom HTML templates can be built using attributes as defined on the Email Templates page to provide processing instructions for the insertion of our dynamic and personalized content.

For those who are not familiar with creating HTML emails, we recommend that they reach out to our partners at MailBakery for help with their custom-designed email templates.

If you have any questions about Custom HTML Templates, please reach out to our Customer Success team at support@rasa.io.
