Image Sizes by Template

Our standard templates use two image display sizes:

Small Images (150x150px display)
  • Classic Template: All articles use small images
  • Insights Template: All articles use small images
  • Newspaper Template: All articles except the featured/first article use small images
Large Images (600x450px display)
  • Big and Bold Template: All articles use large images
  • Newspaper Template: Featured/first article only uses large image
Recommended Upload Sizes

While images display at smaller dimensions in the newsletter, we recommend uploading larger files to ensure quality and resolution:

  • For small image slots: Upload images at minimum 400x400px
  • For large image slots: Upload images at minimum 600x450px

Note: You can use 600x450px for all image uploads if you prefer consistency - the system will automatically crop them appropriately.

Managing Image Warnings
Finding Articles with Small Images

Use the Image Warning filter on the Upcoming Articles page to quickly identify articles that have automatically imported with images below our recommended sizes.

Fixing Image Warnings

When you see an image warning, you have three options to replace the image:

  1. Upload a correctly sized image manually
  2. Select an image from your Image Library
  3. Generate a new image using AI

Tip: You can use the Image Warning filter to generate AI images in bulk for multiple articles at once.
