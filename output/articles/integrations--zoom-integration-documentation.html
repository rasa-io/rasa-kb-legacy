Zoom Integration Documentation

Your webinar strategy just got smarter.You can now seamlessly connect Zoom with rasa.io to automatically pull your webinars into your newsletter!

⚠️ This integration is available by request. If you'd like to enable it, please contact our Customer Success team at support@rasa.io.

Connecting rasa.io and Zoom
  1. Navigate to 'Add Source' or 'Integrations'
###### ###### What Data Comes In from Zoom

When a webinar is synced from Zoom, rasa.io will import the following information:

  • Topic (webinar title)
  • Description
  • Date & Time
  • Registration Link or Invitation Link (see note below)
  • Host

⚠️ Important: If you want to include a registration link in your newsletter, you must enable registration when creating the webinar in your Zoom settings. If registration is not required, rasa.io will instead use the invitation link.

Note: Attributes added via Zoom’s Branding settings (such as custom images or logos) are not available via Zoom’s API. If you’d like to display images for your webinars, these must be manually uploaded in rasa.io.

Disconnecting Zoom and rasa.io

Disconnecting from rasa.io

  1. Navigate to 'Integrations' and select Zoom Integration
  2. Click 'Disconnect'

Disconnecting from Zoom

  1. Log in to your Zoom Account and navigate to the Zoom App Marketplace.
  2. Click Manage >> Added Apps or search for the "rasa.io app for Zoom Integration" app.
  3. Click the "rasa.io app for Zoom Integration" app.
  4. Click Remove.
