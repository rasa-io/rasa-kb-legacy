Introducing t-rex: Your New Content Discovery bot

The latest enhancement in content curation from rasa.io is t-rex, our powerful new chatbot designed to revolutionize how you find and curate content for your newsletters.

What is t-rex?

t-rex is a chatbot developed to assist you in discovering content for your newsletter. 

How to Use t-rex

  1. Accessing t-rex: Navigate to the t-rex chatbot here. You can initiate a conversation with t-rex by clicking on one of the prompts or asking a question directly to t-rex.


2. Starting a Conversation: Begin by using the prompts or asking t-rex about specific topics, keywords, or content types you're interested in. For example, you can say, "I need the latest news on renewable energy" or "Show me top articles about digital marketing trends."
3. Refining Your Search: As you interact with t-rex, provide additional details or preferences to narrow down the content suggestions. t-rex will adjust its recommendations based on your input to better match your needs.
4. Review and Select Content: Review the content recommendations provided by t-rex. You can select articles that best fit your newsletter’s content and audience. t-rex allows you to save and organize your choices for easy integration into your newsletter.
5. Feedback and Improvement: Provide feedback on the recommendations to help t-rex refine its suggestions. The more you interact with t-rex, the better it will understand your preferences and improve its content recommendations.

Tips for Maximizing T-Rex’s Potential

  • Be Specific: The more detailed your queries, the more accurate t-rex's recommendations will be. Include specific topics, industries, or content types in your requests.

Pro Tip: Provide a URL of an article that is on brand for your newsletter to help t-rex find other content to suggest.
- Regular Interaction: Engage with t-rex regularly to stay updated with the latest content trends and ensure your recommendations remain relevant

Support and Resources

If you have any questions or need assistance with t-rex, our support team is here to help. Visit our Support Center or contact us directly at support@rasa.io for personalized support.
